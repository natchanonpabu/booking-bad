# Winner Court — Canonical Decision Register

72 open decisions extracted from `prd.md`, `ia.md`, `ds.md`, `model.md`, `gaps.md`. Rule applied throughout: **where the 48 mockups agree with each other and disagree with the PRD, the mockups win and the PRD is amended.** Rows marked *"Already decided — ratify"* need a signature, not a debate. Every row commits to an answer.

Deciders: **F**=founder · **VO**=venue owner · **P**=product · **E**=engineering · **L**=legal/accounting.
